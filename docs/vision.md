# Avenelix Vision

## North star

Build Avenelix into a technology/product studio capable of taking useful ideas from **problem → AI/software system → production → product**.

The long-term ambition is not simply to sell development hours. The stronger possibility is to build reusable technology and SaaS products under the Avenelix brand while using engineering/client work, when useful, to learn about real problems and generate cash flow.

## What Avenelix should feel like

Avenelix should feel:

- technically capable
- modern but not trend-chasing
- product-minded
- engineering-first
- intelligent without being pretentious
- small and agile initially
- capable of growing into a serious software company

## What it is not

At the current stage, Avenelix is not:

- a large agency
- a mature SaaS company
- a fake enterprise brand
- a generic AI wrapper
- a personal resume website
- an environmental/climate company

## Possible business layers

### 1. Technology / engineering services

Potentially provide AI/software engineering for clients where there is a real problem worth solving.

### 2. Internal products

Use recurring problems and observed demand to identify products worth building.

### 3. SaaS

Eventually launch independent SaaS products under Avenelix if validation supports them.

The layers are not mutually exclusive. The exact model should be driven by evidence, not assumptions.

## Product philosophy

Prefer products that solve a concrete problem and have a clear user and value proposition.

AI should be used because it improves the product, not because the label “AI” makes the idea sound modern.

## Decision filter

Before building a serious Avenelix product, answer:

1. Who has the problem?
2. How painful/frequent is it?
3. What do they do today?
4. Why is the current solution inadequate?
5. What can Avenelix do materially better?
6. Does AI/software create a real advantage?
7. Can a small team build and operate it?
8. Who pays and why?
9. What is the cheapest credible validation?
10. What evidence would make us stop?

## Current objective

Do not jump prematurely to a huge SaaS architecture. First establish the brand, finish the public site, explore problems, validate demand and choose a narrow first product.
